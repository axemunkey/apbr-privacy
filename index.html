APBR Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

APBR is an app for tracking your Powerball tickets and checking them against official results. It is built to keep your information on your device. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

The short version

APBR does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Camera

APBR uses the camera only when you choose to scan a ticket. The image is read on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition. Ticket images are never uploaded or sent anywhere.

Your tickets

Tickets you scan or enter, including their numbers, draw dates, results, and images, are stored only on your device. Deleting a ticket, or deleting the app, removes that data.

Internet access

APBR downloads public information so it can check your tickets:

  • Winning Powerball numbers from the New York State open-data service (data.ny.gov).
  • The current jackpot amount from powerball.com.

These requests ask only for public draw results. They do not include your ticket numbers or any information about you. As with any website, these services may see your device's IP address when the app connects to them, under their own privacy policies.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, APBR alerts you about winning tickets and tickets close to expiring. These alerts are created on your device. No notification server is involved.

Children

APBR is intended for adults who are legally able to purchase lottery tickets. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted as an issue at github.com/axemunkey/apbr-privacy.
